# Using the calculator

Enter amounts in Canadian dollars. Monthly spending repeats across twelve modeled billing cycles.

## Choose cards and settings

Select a card to see its fee, rates, cap notes, and issuer links. Tangerine permits two selected bonus categories, or three when the savings-deposit option is enabled. The selected card and baseline have independent settings.

**Cover the fee** finds spending where reward value covers the fee. **Beat a no-fee card** subtracts baseline rewards from selected-card rewards after fees. Each category row assumes spending only in that category. EQ is prepaid, and PC Financial, Triangle and Scene+ use points or store rewards rather than cash.

For PC Financial and Scene+, choose store/travel redemption value or the lower statement-credit value. Set fuel price when modeling per-litre rewards, and partner-store tax for Triangle. Rogers has a qualifying-service checkbox. Wealthsimple has an ongoing fee-waiver checkbox. Settings apply independently to each card.

The Monthly/Annual toggle changes category threshold units. Budget fields remain monthly.

## Enter your budget

Count each purchase once: do not enter restaurant delivery under both dining and delivery. Fields accept $0 through $1,000,000 per month. Invalid values show an error and suppress personal results.

Keep Costco gas separate from other gas and EV charging, and public transit separate from taxis/rideshares. Use Costco warehouse rather than groceries for warehouse purchases. Put recurring subscriptions in bills only once. See [Card catalog](./card-catalog.md) for the merchant-code assumptions.

**Try an example budget** loads bundled values. **Reset** clears them. Reloading also clears inputs and settings.

Use the Loblaw, Sobeys-family, Shoppers, Home Hardware and Cineplex fields instead of their general-category fields for qualifying purchases. Keep Walmart grocery-coded purchases separate so Triangle can exclude them. Use the Triangle partner-store field for eligible purchases at its participating retail brands, with tax included. Utility inputs assume qualifying utility/telecom merchant codes. General recurring bills remain separate. Put each fuel purchase in only one fuel-chain field.

## Read results

Annual reward value is reward before fees; rewards after fee subtracts the annual fee. For points cards, these are estimated redemption values, not cash payments. Your personal threshold scales the spending mix without changing its proportions.

**No break-even** means the modeled card never reaches the target under the chosen mix. **Already covered** means no spending is required. Empty budgets prompt you to enter spending because they have no category proportions.

A **Falls behind** message reports a later crossing: bonus caps can make a card initially win, then lose against the baseline. See [Calculation model](./calculation-model.md).
